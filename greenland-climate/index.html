From regional field to local detail

Climate-model files hold spatial information that a single temperature curve cannot show. This plate connects a Greenland-wide view with a regional crop, making the variation within the distributed field visible before extracting a point series.

The figure uses the supplied annual T2M field directly, with no added terrain shading. Its fine detail belongs to a prepared 1 km product; that grid spacing does not imply that the atmospheric model was simulated at 1 km.

Processing and presentation

The Python workflow reads the NetCDF with xarray, interprets the annual time coordinate, and plots the temperature array in its native EPSG:3413 coordinates. Preserving the projected grid avoids the distortion of displaying Greenland on equally scaled longitude and latitude axes.

The regional panel uses a new 300 × 300 km crop. A shared temperature scale permits direct comparison between the two panels.

From spatial fields
to future trajectories

The same gridded climate products can be reduced to compact local time series. This comparison uses downloaded annual MAR near-surface temperature extracts for three emissions pathways over their shared 2030–2100 period.

Eight series pass the common-location check, giving 568 annual values. Separate panels keep scenario differences readable, while shared axes preserve the temperature comparison.

Large rasters,
compact evidence

01 / Extract

Reduce at source

The download workflow reads each source NetCDF with xarray, selects a grid cell from its geographic coordinates and saves a compact extract. It verifies the output before removing the downloaded full-grid file.

02 / Combine

Preserve provenance

Annual extracts are grouped by model, scenario and location. Calendar-aware conversion retains the original time metadata; records are sorted and duplicate observations checked before concatenation.

03 / Compare

Validate before plotting

Check units, finite values, unique annual timestamps, complete year coverage and a consistent selected grid cell. Plot the shared period without smoothing, filling gaps or joining to a historical baseline.

Model coverage and interpretation

SSP1–2.6 includes CESM2 and MPI-ESM1-2-HR forcings. SSP2–4.5 and SSP5–8.5 also include UKESM1-0-LL. Unequal model coverage means differences between scenario medians reflect both scenario and ensemble composition. The median is a descriptive summary, not a confidence interval.

Two downloaded NorESM2 series use a selected grid cell approximately 1 km from the other eight. They are excluded from this common-location comparison. The spatial plate above is a separate NorESM2 field example; its highlighted regional crop is not presented as the extraction location.

The plotted variable is T2M in degrees Celsius, read directly from annual files. Model-generated annual records may have midyear timestamps; they are shown by year, not interpreted as July observations.
